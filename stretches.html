Stretch Library

Find a comfortable stretch for the muscles you trained today.

LEGS

Standing Quad Stretch

Stand tall, bend one knee, and gently bring your heel toward your glutes. Hold a wall for balance if needed.

Hold: 20–30 seconds per side

LEGS

Hamstring Stretch

Extend one leg with your heel on the floor and gently hinge forward at your hips, keeping your back comfortable.

Hold: 20–30 seconds per side

GLUTES

Figure-Four Stretch

Lie on your back, cross one ankle over the opposite thigh, and gently draw the supporting leg toward you.

Hold: 20–30 seconds per side

GLUTES

Knees-to-Chest Stretch

Lie on your back and gently bring your knees toward your chest without forcing the movement.

Hold: 20–30 seconds

BACK

Child's Pose

Kneel, sit your hips back toward your heels, and reach your arms forward as far as feels comfortable.

Hold: 20–30 seconds

BACK

Cat-Cow Stretch

On your hands and knees, slowly round your back and then gently arch it while breathing steadily.

Repeat: 6–8 slow cycles

UPPER BODY

Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch

Bring one arm across your chest and gently support it with the opposite arm.

Hold: 20–30 seconds per side

UPPER BODY

Chest Opener

Stand tall, bring your arms gently behind you, and open your chest without arching your lower back.

Hold: 20–30 seconds

CORE

Gentle Cobra Stretch

Lie on your stomach and gently lift your chest using your arms only as much as feels comfortable.

Hold: 10–20 seconds

CORE

Side Stretch

Stand tall, raise one arm, and lean gently to the opposite side without twisting.

Hold: 20 seconds per side
